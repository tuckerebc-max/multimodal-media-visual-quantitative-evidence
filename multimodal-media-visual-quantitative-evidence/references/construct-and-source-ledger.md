# Construct and source ledger

**Registry:** `EXP-MEDIA` / `COMP-MEDIA-EVIDENCE`  
**Design status:** `READY_WITH_CONDITIONS`  
**Project snapshot:** 2026-08-11

## Construct boundary

The competency is evidence-centered interpretation and use across text, image, chart, map, audio, video, interface, and composite media. Representation features, rhetorical effects, and evidence validity are separate. Quantitative checks must preserve units, denominator, scale, population, selection, transformation, and uncertainty.

## Release conditions

Use original/open or rights-cleared charts, images, audio, video, and transcripts. Close open gates for cross-domain performance evidence, edit-provenance fixtures, accessible equivalents, and domain-specific scientific, legal, journalistic, and civic rules.
